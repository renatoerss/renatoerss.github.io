Selected work

Examples of four projects, told as problem, approach and outcome.

01

How a chemical tag changes a global regulator

Boston Children’s Hospital · Harvard Medical School · 2022–2026

Problem
CRP controls hundreds of genes in Vibrio cholerae. We wanted to know how it partners with PepA and H-NS, and what modifications to CRP do to the cell’s stress response. [Draft: rewrite in your own words]
Approach
RNA-seq and ChIP-seq analysis in R, mass-spec mapping of CRP modifications, and structural models of the complexes (AlphaFold, HADDOCK) to choose which residues to mutate. [Draft]
Outcome
Two PNAS papers: sequestration of a dual-function DNA-binding protein by CRP (2022), and formylation of CRP activating region 3 and the response to oxidative DNA damage (2026). [Draft]

02

A gene that looked essential, until the conditions changed

University of São Paulo with Vanderbilt University · 2017–2021

Problem
fur seemed essential in Chromobacterium violaceum, which blocked the study of iron and zinc homeostasis in infection. [Draft]
Approach
Selection under iron chelation to recover mutants, transposon mutagenesis to find suppressors, phenotypic assays, and calprotectin assays standardized with the Skaar and Chazin labs. [Draft]
Outcome
Papers in Applied and Environmental Microbiology (2020) and Infection and Immunity (2021). [Draft]

03

Building the switch before using it

University of São Paulo · 2015–2017

Problem
Studying essential genes in Leishmania major needs conditional control that the field lacked. [Draft]
Approach
A rapamycin-inducible diCre-LoxP system built by homologous recombination, then applied to conditional truncation of Rad9. [Draft]
Outcome
Dose- and time-dependent induction, published in Mol Biochem Parasitol (2017) and as a Springer methods chapter (2019). [Draft]
diCre-LoxPFlow cytometryPhyre2
04

Making a messy protocol repeatable

Boston Children’s Hospital · Harvard Medical School · 2022–2026

Problem
Localization and vesicle work needed clean fractions and quantitative blots that other people could reproduce. [Draft]
Approach
Four iterations of a fractionation and sucrose-gradient workflow, vesicle tracking with ZetaView, quantitative NIR Westerns, all written up as SOPs. [Draft]
Outcome
50+ SOPs in LabArchives with QC benchmarks, used to onboard junior scientists. [Draft]
